# Driver Guide

Accepting jobs, buying materials, and getting paid.

## What you need

- A phone with a browser. The driver app is a web app — **nothing to install**.
- Your login (email and password). The dispatcher sets this up and gives you the initial password.
- A way to take photos — the app uses your phone's camera.

Add the app to your home screen for quick access.

---

## Getting a job

When a job is available, you get a **text message** and it appears in your **Job Board**.

Each job shows you:

| Shown before you accept | Hidden until you accept |
|---|---|
| The store and its address | The customer's delivery address |
| How many items | The customer's contact and drop-off instructions |
| Your payout | |
| Any special notes | |

You can see enough to decide, but the customer's location is not exposed until you commit. This is deliberate.

### Accepting

Tap **Accept**. The job is yours.

**It is first-come, first-served.** If two drivers tap at the same moment, exactly one wins and the other is told immediately that it's taken. You will never be assigned a job someone else already has, and you will never drive to a store for a job that isn't yours.

### If you can't take it

Tap **Decline**. Nothing happens to you — declines are not held against you and are not reported individually to the dispatcher.

## Doing the job

### 1. Go to the store

The job card shows the store address. The address is also in your phone's maps app — tap it to navigate.

> **Check the store carefully.** The store was selected by the dispatcher, not by the system, and the cart email does not tell us which store the customer used. If something seems wrong — the items aren't sold here, the store is far from the delivery address — call the dispatcher *before* you buy anything.

### 2. Buy the items

Buy exactly what the job card lists. The customer's cart is a **cart, not an order** — they never purchased it. You are making the purchase.

Pay with the company card or method the dispatcher told you about.

**Keep the receipt.** You will photograph it.

### 3. Photograph the receipt

After loading, mark **Items Purchased** and photograph the receipt. This is the evidence behind the charge.

You can skip it, but you must give a reason, and the dispatcher will see it. Don't make a habit of it.

### 4. Head out

Mark **En Route**. The customer is automatically emailed that you're on the way.

### 5. Deliver

At the site, follow the drop-off instructions on the job card. If no one is there and the instructions say drop-off, leave the materials where instructed.

Mark **Delivered** and photograph the delivery. This is the customer's proof the materials arrived. Skipping it requires a reason.

> If a handoff is required and nobody is available, call the dispatcher. Do not leave materials if the instructions say to hand off.

## Status buttons, in order

| Button | When |
|---|---|
| **At Store** | You've arrived |
| **Items Purchased** | After buying and loading — photograph the receipt |
| **En Route** | You're driving to the site |
| **Delivered** | Materials are delivered — photograph the drop-off |

You can only move forward. If you tap the wrong one, tell the dispatcher — they can correct it.

---

## Getting paid

The customer pays the platform, not you. Your payout is tracked per job.

The job card shows your payout amount before you accept, so there are no surprises.

---

## Quick reference

**Before you accept:** check the store and the item count. If the store looks wrong, don't accept.

**Before you buy:** confirm the items are actually at that store. The cart is a request, not a reservation — an item may be out of stock. If something is missing, call the dispatcher before substituting anything.

**Before you leave the store:** photograph the receipt.

**Before you leave the site:** photograph the delivery.

**If anything goes wrong:** call the dispatcher. The app has no way to report a problem mid-job yet, and a phone call is faster than a wrong delivery.
